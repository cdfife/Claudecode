# Chad Fife — Top 20 HR Tech & EdTech Target Companies

Based on your public profile at chadfife.com and LinkedIn, your core strengths are:
- **20+ years** in product marketing, GTM strategy, brand naming, and copywriting
- Deep experience in **assessment & credentialing** (Pearson TalentLens, PSI Services, PAN/AmIJobReady, Examity)
- **Video interviewing** (Talview)
- **Career guidance / workforce readiness** (jobZology — 80+ universities; AmIJobReady — 30+ colleges)
- **Global marketing leadership** (Pearson: 8 countries; PSI: NA/UK/Middle East)
- **Higher ed partnership development** and B2B go-to-market in both HR Tech and EdTech
- Founding team and VC/PE-backed company experience

## HR Tech Companies (10)

---

**Why you're a fit:** You spent time at Talview, a direct video interviewing competitor. You know this category's buyers (TA leaders, CHROs), the AI fairness messaging challenges, and the enterprise sales cycle cold. HireVue is the market leader and continuously needs to differentiate against a wave of AI-native challengers — your category expertise is immediately actionable as a consultant or VP Marketing.

**Best approach:** Consulting (GTM strategy, product messaging) or VP/Director of Marketing role.

**Best approach:** Consulting (product positioning, content, naming) or fractional CMO.

**Best approach:** Consulting on international GTM or product marketing leadership.

**Best approach:** Senior product marketing consulting or VP Marketing role.

### 6. Eightfold AI

**Best approach:** Consulting (GTM strategy, messaging, launch support).

---

### 11. Handshake
**Why you're a fit:** Handshake is the leading career network for students, trusted by 1,600+ educational institutions and 1 million employers. Your jobZology product was used at 80+ universities — you know the higher ed partnership sales motion, the student success buyer, and how to position career readiness tools to institutions. Handshake tripled its ARR in 2025 and is heavily investing in AI features.

**Best approach:** Senior marketing or partnerships consulting, or a Director/VP of Partnerships role.

**Why you're a fit:** You worked at Pearson. You built TalentLens. You ran credentialing and certification marketing at PSI. Credly is the world's leading digital credentialing and badging platform — and it's owned by Pearson. This is arguably the single tightest overlap on this entire list. You speak the language of issuers, earners, and employers simultaneously.

**Best approach:** Consulting (GTM strategy, content, product marketing) or a leadership role — you have Pearson institutional knowledge and credentialing expertise few outsiders do.

### 13. Guild Education
**Why you're a fit:** Guild connects employers with education benefits, sitting exactly at the HR + EdTech intersection where your career has lived. Your dual fluency — talking to HR buyers AND academic/college audiences — is genuinely rare and is what Guild needs to grow enterprise partnerships. They work with large employers (Walmart, Disney) and university partners simultaneously.

**Best approach:** Consulting on B2B marketing strategy, or a VP of Marketing or Partnerships role.

### 14. Lightcast (formerly EMSI Burning Glass)

You've sold workforce readiness solutions to colleges and employers. Lightcast needs marketers who understand both audiences deeply to grow adoption.

**Best approach:** Consulting (go-to-market for higher ed and employer segments) or senior marketing role.

### 15. EAB
**Why you're a fit:** EAB provides research, technology, and consulting to colleges and universities on enrollment, student success, and workforce outcomes. Your 30+ college distribution for AmIJobReady and 80+ university reach with jobZology means you've navigated higher ed sales cycles and institutional partnerships firsthand. EAB needs marketing leaders with real higher ed credibility — not just general B2B experience.

**Best approach:** Consulting engagement or VP of Marketing / Thought Leadership role.

---

### 16. Parchment (part of Instructure/Canvas)
**Why you're a fit:** Parchment is the leading digital credential, transcript, and learner record platform. Your career intersects perfectly: credentialing (PSI, Pearson), higher education (AmIJobReady, jobZology), and digital verification. As verifiable credentials grow in importance for both HR and EdTech, Parchment needs marketing leadership who can bridge both buyer worlds.

**Best approach:** Consulting on product positioning and B2B content strategy.

---

### 17. Cengage / Cengage Work
**Why you're a fit:** Cengage is one of the largest education publishers, and its Cengage Work division focuses squarely on workforce readiness and career outcomes — your wheelhouse. They compete in the same college-to-career space where you launched AmIJobReady and jobZology. As a large company continuously launching new workforce products, they're a strong fit for a consulting engagement or internal product marketing role.

**Best approach:** Consulting (product launch, GTM, naming) or VP Product Marketing.

### 18. Wiley / mthree
**Why you're a fit:** Wiley's workforce education division and its mthree subsidiary (trains and places tech talent globally) operate in international markets you know — particularly the UK and India, both areas where you've led marketing (PSI's UK team, Talview's India team). Their global footprint + workforce training focus = direct overlap with your experience.

**Best approach:** Consulting on international marketing strategy or fractional CMO for workforce division.

---

### 19. Riipen
**Why you're a fit:** Riipen connects universities with employers through work-integrated learning (real projects for students). This is the intersection of higher ed partnerships, employer relationships, and workforce readiness — three pillars of your career. They're growing fast and need experienced marketers who understand both sides of the two-sided marketplace you've spent 20 years serving.

**Best approach:** Consulting (GTM, content marketing, partnership marketing) — strong fit for project-based engagement.

### 20. Stepful
**Why you're a fit:** Stepful was named the #1 EdTech company in the US for 2025 by TIME Magazine and focuses on healthcare workforce training — helping people get into healthcare careers fast. You have Nurse.com in your background, which means you've marketed to healthcare professionals and understand that audience. Stepful is growing fast, VC-backed, and is exactly the type of early-growth company where a seasoned product marketing consultant has the most impact.
