## Key Market Trends (2025-2026)

- **Agentic AI** is the dominant theme — autonomous AI agents that perform multi-step HR tasks (e.g., Wisq's Harper, G-P Gia)
- AI adoption in HR has **doubled to 42%** of organizations; H1 2025 saw **$3B+ in HR tech funding** (60% increase vs. 2024)
- **Unified platforms** (HR + IT + Finance) like Rippling are gaining over point solutions
- **Skills-based hiring** and workforce intelligence are replacing traditional role-based models
- **Global employment infrastructure** (Deel, Remote, G-P) is maturing rapidly as remote work persists
