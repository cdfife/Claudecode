Chad Fife — Top 20 HR Tech & EdTech Target Companies

Profile strengths this list is based on:
  • 20+ years in product marketing, GTM strategy, brand naming, and copywriting
  • Deep experience in assessment & credentialing (Pearson TalentLens, PSI Services, PAN/AmIJobReady, Examity)
  • Video interviewing (Talview)
  • Career guidance / workforce readiness (jobZology — 80+ universities; AmIJobReady — 30+ colleges)
  • Global marketing leadership (Pearson: 8 countries; PSI: NA/UK/Middle East)
  • Higher ed partnership development and B2B go-to-market in both HR Tech and EdTech
  • Founding team and VC/PE-backed company experience

HR Tech Companies (10)

1. HireVue

You spent time at Talview, a direct video interviewing competitor. You know this category's buyers (TA leaders, CHROs), the AI fairness messaging challenges, and the enterprise sales cycle cold. HireVue is the market leader and continuously needs to differentiate against a wave of AI-native challengers — your category expertise is immediately actionable.

Best approach: Consulting (GTM strategy, product messaging) or VP/Director of Marketing role.

2. Criteria Corp

Criteria Corp's pre-employment testing portfolio (cognitive, personality, skills) is the closest public-market parallel to TalentLens (Pearson) and PSI's assessment suite. You've lived the product, the buyer, and the competitive landscape. They serve SMBs up to enterprise — and your experience positioning assessments for broad markets makes you highly credible.

Best approach: Consulting (product positioning, content, naming) or fractional CMO.

3. Harver (formerly Pymetrics)

Harver absorbed Pymetrics' neuroscience-based game assessments and now competes in the same talent assessment space where you built TalentLens and ran PSI. They operate in 42 languages — and you've led marketing across 8 countries for Pearson. Your ability to internationalize assessment marketing is rare.

Best approach: Consulting on international GTM or product marketing leadership.

4. Paradox (Olivia)

Paradox is one of the fastest-growing AI recruiting companies, known for its conversational AI assistant Olivia. They need product marketers who can make complex AI capabilities feel accessible to HR buyers — exactly the translation work you've done throughout your career. VC-backed and expanding rapidly.

Best approach: Consulting (product launch, messaging, naming) — they regularly launch new products that need clear positioning.

5. Phenom

Phenom is a large, PE-backed talent experience platform covering the full talent lifecycle. They have a big product surface area and constantly need product marketing support to help buyers understand what they do. Your background bridging assessment, video, and career guidance gives you cross-product credibility rare in HR tech marketers.

Best approach: Senior product marketing consulting or VP Marketing role.

6. Eightfold AI

Eightfold built a skills intelligence platform that overlaps with both your assessment (PSI/Pearson) and career guidance (jobZology) experience. They work with large enterprises and need marketers who understand talent intelligence — not just software. Their AI narrative is complex and needs the kind of clear positioning and copy you specialize in.

Best approach: Consulting (GTM strategy, messaging, launch support).

7. Mercer | Mettl

Mettl (now part of Mercer/Marsh McLennan) is a global assessment platform — functionally the same business as PSI's assessment division, where you were VP of Global Marketing over NA/UK/Middle East. You've already run this playbook internationally.

Best approach: Consulting or fractional marketing leadership for their global expansion.

8. Degreed

Degreed bridges corporate learning and skills intelligence — sitting squarely at the intersection of HR Tech and EdTech, which is exactly where your career has lived. Their 2025 HR Tech Award winner product (Degreed Maestro) needs clear product marketing to enterprise buyers. You can speak to both skills assessment and learning outcomes credibly.

Best approach: Consulting (product launch, content strategy) or VP Marketing.

9. iMocha

iMocha is a fast-growing AI skills intelligence platform for workforce assessment. It's a category you know better than almost any marketer — pre-employment testing + AI + skills mapping. They're scaling globally and need experienced product marketing leadership who has sold assessment solutions to enterprise buyers before.

Best approach: Consulting or fractional CMO as they scale in North America.

10. Checkr

Checkr is expanding from background screening into a broader pre-hire intelligence platform (Fast Company Most Innovative HR 2026). They're moving into adjacent assessment territory you know well, and need product marketing that can reframe them beyond background checks.

Best approach: Consulting on product marketing and GTM for new product lines.

EdTech Companies (10)

11. Handshake

Handshake is the leading career network for students, trusted by 1,600+ educational institutions and 1 million employers. Your jobZology product was used at 80+ universities — you know the higher ed partnership sales motion, the student success buyer, and how to position career readiness tools to institutions. Handshake tripled its ARR in 2025.

Best approach: Senior marketing or partnerships consulting, or a Director/VP of Partnerships role.

12. Credly (a Pearson company)

You worked at Pearson. You built TalentLens. You ran credentialing and certification marketing at PSI. Credly is the world's leading digital credentialing and badging platform — and it's owned by Pearson. This is arguably the single tightest overlap on this entire list. You speak the language of issuers, earners, and employers simultaneously.

Best approach: Consulting (GTM strategy, content, product marketing) or a leadership role.

13. Guild Education

Guild connects employers with education benefits, sitting exactly at the HR + EdTech intersection where your career has lived. Your dual fluency — talking to HR buyers AND academic/college audiences — is genuinely rare. They work with large employers (Walmart, Disney) and university partners simultaneously.

Best approach: Consulting on B2B marketing strategy, or a VP of Marketing or Partnerships role.

14. Lightcast (formerly EMSI Burning Glass)

Lightcast is the dominant labor market intelligence platform — its core use cases (workforce readiness, career pathway mapping, skills gap analysis) are exactly what AmIJobReady and jobZology addressed. You've sold workforce readiness solutions to colleges and employers.

Best approach: Consulting (go-to-market for higher ed and employer segments) or senior marketing role.

15. EAB

EAB provides research, technology, and consulting to colleges and universities on enrollment, student success, and workforce outcomes. Your 30+ college distribution for AmIJobReady and 80+ university reach with jobZology means you've navigated higher ed sales cycles and institutional partnerships firsthand.

Best approach: Consulting engagement or VP of Marketing / Thought Leadership role.

16. Parchment (part of Instructure/Canvas)

Parchment is the leading digital credential, transcript, and learner record platform. Your career intersects perfectly: credentialing (PSI, Pearson), higher education (AmIJobReady, jobZology), and digital verification. As verifiable credentials grow in importance for both HR and EdTech, Parchment needs marketing leadership who can bridge both buyer worlds.

Best approach: Consulting on product positioning and B2B content strategy.

17. Cengage / Cengage Work

Cengage's Cengage Work division focuses squarely on workforce readiness and career outcomes — your wheelhouse. They compete in the same college-to-career space where you launched AmIJobReady and jobZology. As a large company continuously launching new workforce products, they're a strong fit for a consulting engagement.

Best approach: Consulting (product launch, GTM, naming) or VP Product Marketing.

18. Wiley / mthree

Wiley's workforce education division and its mthree subsidiary operate in international markets you know — particularly the UK and India, both areas where you've led marketing (PSI's UK team, Talview's India team). Their global footprint + workforce training focus = direct overlap with your experience.

Best approach: Consulting on international marketing strategy or fractional CMO for workforce division.

19. Riipen

Riipen connects universities with employers through work-integrated learning. This is the intersection of higher ed partnerships, employer relationships, and workforce readiness — three pillars of your career. They're growing fast and need experienced marketers who understand both sides of the two-sided marketplace you've spent 20 years serving.

Best approach: Consulting (GTM, content marketing, partnership marketing).

20. Stepful

#1 EdTech company in the US for 2025 (TIME Magazine). Focuses on healthcare workforce training. You have Nurse.com in your background — healthcare + workforce + EdTech is a rare combination. Stepful is VC-backed and at exactly the growth stage where a seasoned product marketing consultant has the most impact.

Best approach: Consulting (product marketing, launch strategy, GTM).
